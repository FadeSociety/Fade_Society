What your shop gets

Online booking page

Customers pick a time and leave a name and phone number. The booking shows up on your list.

QR code for walk-ins

Stick it at the entrance. Walk-ins scan it, enter their name and join the queue from their own phone.

One list for your chair

Booked and walk-in customers are marked differently. You see the free time before the next booking and decide if a walk-in fits.

A page for your work

Your shop gets its own page with photos of your best cuts, so new customers see your work before they visit.

How it works in your shop

1

Put the QR at the door

We set up your page and a QR code ready to print. Stick it where people wait.

2

Customers book or scan

Regulars book a time online. Walk-ins scan the QR and join the queue.

3

You see one list

Check the free time before the next booking. Let a walk-in in, or ask them to wait a little.
